What We Do

Roofing & siding, done right.

Residential and commercial — from a single leak to a full re-roof, every project gets three generations of family experience behind it.

Metal Roofing

Standing-seam metal panels engineered for prairie winters. Lifetime materials, 50+ year lifespan, and premium curb appeal.

Asphalt Shingles

The Saskatchewan standard — cost-effective, wide range of styles and colors, backed by manufacturer and workmanship warranty.

Rubber Roofing

EPDM and torch-on membranes for flat and low-slope roofs. Ideal for commercial buildings, garages, and additions.

Roof Repairs

Leaks, storm damage, missing shingles, flashing failures — we diagnose and fix fast, before small problems become big ones.

Siding, Soffit & Fascia

Vinyl, aluminum, or steel siding, plus soffit and fascia. Complete exterior packages that match your new roof.

Drywall & Interior

Drywall install and repair, wall finishing, patchwork. The interior side of the same job — one crew, one bill.
