Our Story

Roofing runs in our family.

Grandfather taught father. Father taught son. That's how DNA Roofing was built, one roof at a time, from the same crew that's been on Saskatchewan rooftops for three generations.

We don't roll into town in a rented truck. We live here. Our families are here. And when we hand you the keys back after a job, our name is still the one you'll see across the street next winter, still standing behind it.

That's why we don't cut corners on flashing. That's why every job gets a workmanship warranty. And that's why, when a shingle lets go five years out, you can call the same phone number and get the same crew back on your roof.
